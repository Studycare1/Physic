Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

StudyCare is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our test platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you take a test, we automatically collect certain technical information to improve our services, including your device model, operating system, IP address, and general location (City/Country).

The information collected is used solely to analyze test performance, track exam progress, and ensure the security of the platform. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Test Data

Your answers, time taken, and clicks during the test are stored securely to provide you with a result and detailed analysis.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official channels.
